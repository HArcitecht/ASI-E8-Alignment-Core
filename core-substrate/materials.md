# Core Substrate & Material Specifications (CSMS-0.1)

## 1. Physical Containment & Thermal Stabilization Matrix

### 1.1 Superfluid Helium-4 (Helium II) Environment
To maintain absolute quantum coherence across the decentralized honeycomb grid, the entire primary infrastructure is submerged within a zero-viscosity Superfluid Helium-4 (Helium II) thermal bath.
- **Operational Temperature:** $T \le 2.17\text{ K}$ (Lambda point stabilization)
- **Thermal Conductivity:** Near-infinite, preventing any localized thermal blooming from lithographic modulation shifts.

### 1.2 Non-Linear Auxetic Titanium Framework
The structural boundaries of each individual hexagon cell are fabricated from a specialized auxetic titanium matrix. Unlike classical materials that thin out when stretched, this matrix expands in all directions when subjected to longitudinal tension, providing unprecedented kinetic shock absorption against external macro-impacts.
